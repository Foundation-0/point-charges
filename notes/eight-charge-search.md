# Eight-charge construction pilot

The seven-charge certificate is an input, not a solution of the eight-or-more problem. The generator puts a new rational site at the exact midpoint of a printed index-one enclosure and varies a tiny positive charge over three dyadic scales. The new site is a proposed rational point, not an asserted exact equilibrium.

No numerical roots have been counted for these proposals. A finite screen must record every failure and Morse-inconsistent case. For eight positive charges with nondegenerate equilibria, the Morse relation gives an odd total count, so the first possible total above 49 is 51. A numerical value above the threshold needs a complete independent certificate. No inference about global maximality follows from a failed search.

The prior seven-charge result does not resolve the open problem for eight or more charges.
